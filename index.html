Biography

I am a lecturer at Wenzhou University (温州大学). I received my Ph.D. from the School of Computer Science, Fudan University (2021.9–2025.6). My research focuses on multimodal large language models, world models, and multi-agent systems, with applications in general video understanding, medical imaging, industrial inspection, and sports analytics. During my doctoral studies, I was guided by Academician Qionghai Dai from Tsinghua University and Professor Xiangyang Xue from Fudan University.

🔬 Research Interests

I study how multimodal foundation models can perceive, reason about, and act upon complex visual worlds—across general, medical, industrial, and sports settings.

1. Multimodal Large Language Models: Design and Applications

Designing multimodal LLMs that ground language in visual and temporal signals, and transferring these models to high-stakes application domains with reliable understanding and localization.

General vision–language understanding

  • Video understanding and temporal reasoning
  • Video question answering
  • Visual grounding and temporal localization

Medical imaging and surgical intelligence

  • Whole-slide image (WSI) analysis
  • CT analysis and medical image segmentation
  • Surgical video understanding and skill assessment

Industrial perception

  • Intelligent visual inspection
  • Anomalous event monitoring

Sports analytics

  • Athlete action quality assessment

2. World Models

Learning predictive models of environmental dynamics to support temporal foresight, planning, and decision-making in multimodal settings.

3. Multi-Agent Systems

Coordinating multiple agents for collaborative perception, reasoning, and task execution in complex environments.
