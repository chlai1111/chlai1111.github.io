Biography

I am a lecturer at Wenzhou University (温州大学). I received my Ph.D. from the School of Computer Science, Fudan University (2021.9–2025.6). My research interests include computer vision, machine learning, video understanding, and multi-modal knowledge extraction and reasoning. During my doctoral studies, I was guided by Academician Qionghai Dai from Tsinghua University and Professor Xiangyang Xue from Fudan University.
